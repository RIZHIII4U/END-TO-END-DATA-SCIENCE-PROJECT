*DESCRIPTION*: Task-3: Foodie Saver is a comprehensive food discovery and ordering platform that goes beyond the ordinary. It seamlessly integrates three key elements — price comparison for food ordering, mood-based meal suggestions, and ingredient-based recipe creation. Whether you're craving the ease of ordering, seeking culinary inspiration, or
planning a home-cooked meal, Foodie Saver is an all-in-one solution.

Objective:

Enhanced Food Discovery: Simplify the process of discovering new and exciting dishes tailored to your mood and preferences. Effortless Ordering: Compare prices across popular food ordering platforms to ensure you get the best value for your money.
Creative Cooking: Make cooking at home an enjoyable experience by receiving personalized recipes based on the ingredients you have on hand.

Goals:

User Satisfaction: Prioritize user satisfaction by offering a userfriendly interface, personalized recommendations, and reliable price information.
Market Impact: Establish Foodie Saver as a prominent player in the food tech industry, filling the gaps left by existing solutions. Culinary Exploration: Inspire users to explore new flavours, cuisines, and cooking techniques, fostering a sense of culinary adventure.
Partnerships: Form strategic partnerships with popular food ordering platforms to provide users with an extensive and diverse range of choices.
